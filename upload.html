Upload Tutorials

🖼️ Supported Formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and other common image formats.

Using Imgur (Recommended)
1. Visit imgur.com/upload
2. Drag and drop your image or click to browse
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Right-click the image and select "Copy Image Address"
5. Paste the direct link into the Image Link field above
🖼️ Using Google Drive
1. Upload your image to Google Drive
2. Right-click the uploaded file and choose "Get shareable link"
3. Ensure sharing is set to "Anyone with the link can view"
4. Copy the link and modify it: Replace the end with "/view?usp=sharing" to "/uc?id=FILE_ID"
5. Paste the modified link into the Image Link field above
🖼️ Other Hosting Services & Troubleshooting
Other Services:
• Use any reliable image hosting service that provides direct links
• Avoid temporary links that expire
• Test the link in a new browser tab to ensure it loads correctly

Troubleshooting:
• If the image doesn't load, check if the link is direct and accessible
• Some sites block hotlinking; use a hosting service instead
🎥 YouTube Videos
• Standard URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
• Short URL: https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID
• With timestamp: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID&t=30
• Playlist URLs are not supported; use the direct video URL
🎥 General Tips & Troubleshooting
How to Get the Link:
1. Open the video in your browser
2. Copy the URL from the address bar
3. Paste it directly into the Video Link field above

Embedding Notes:
• Videos will be embedded in prompt cards
• Users can click to play directly in the gallery
• Private or restricted videos won't work

Troubleshooting:
• If validation fails, double-check the URL format
• Ensure the video is public and not age-restricted
• Try refreshing the page if issues persist
